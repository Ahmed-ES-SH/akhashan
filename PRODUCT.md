# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Saudi business owners, HR managers, and individuals seeking reliable overseas manpower recruitment. Their context is high-trust, service-driven decision-making — they are delegating a critical business function (staffing) and need confidence in the provider's credentials, track record, and professionalism.

## Product Purpose

Landing page for Abdullah Khashan Al-Shammari Recruitment Company. Establishes institutional trust, communicates Saudi corporate authority, showcases service breadth (domestic, corporate, specialized recruitment), and drives contact inquiries (phone, WhatsApp, consultation form). Success is measured by visitor confidence and conversion to contact.

## Brand Personality

Authoritative, Modern, Saudi-focused. The voice is official but not bureaucratic, premium but not flashy. Confident, clear, and reassuring — every word earns its place.

## Anti-references

- Brutalism, cyberpunk, heavy glassmorphism, neon palettes
- Playful startup visuals, overdecorated luxury, excessive shadow/blur
- Dense dashboard-like layouts, trendy marketing language
- Stock imagery that looks fake, overly cheerful startup visuals
- Generic corporate templates with no Saudi identity

## Design Principles

1. **Trust first** — every design decision serves visitor confidence. Licenses, certifications, and credentials are prominent, not buried.
2. **Clarity over decoration** — clean hierarchy, generous spacing, purposeful typography. Nothing decorative that doesn't serve communication.
3. **Premium without excess** — gold is an accent, not a theme. Quality cues through spacing, typography, and restraint, not ornamentation.
4. **Saudi identity without cliché** — green and gold rooted in the brand's actual logo, not decorative nationalism. Professional Gulf corporate context.
5. **Strong CTA placement** — contact is the primary action. Every section can lead to it naturally.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG-compliant color contrast (dark green on white, white on dark backgrounds)
- Semantic HTML, proper heading hierarchy
- Reduced motion respected via Framer Motion preferences
- Bilingual layout (Arabic RTL / English LTR) with appropriate font stacks
