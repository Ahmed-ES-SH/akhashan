# Design System & Visual Identity Guide

## Abdullah Khashan Al-Shammari Recruitment Company

**Project:** Landing Page + Future Website Foundation  
**Recommended Style:** **Modern Gulf Corporate Premium**  
**Primary Goal:** Build trust, communicate professionalism, and present the company as a reliable Saudi recruitment partner.

---

## 1) Brand Summary

This company operates in a high-trust, service-driven market: overseas recruitment and manpower services in Saudi Arabia.

The visual identity must communicate:

- Trust
- Formality
- Stability
- Saudi presence
- Operational credibility
- Ease of contact
- Premium but not flashy positioning

This is not a creative agency, an entertainment brand, or a tech startup.  
The design should feel **official, clear, and reassuring**.

---

## 2) Final Style Recommendation

### Chosen Style: **Modern Gulf Corporate Premium**

This is the best fit because it combines:

- **Corporate** structure for trust and clarity
- **Minimal** spacing for readability
- **Bold** typography in key sections for authority
- **Premium** accents for quality and prestige
- **Saudi identity** through color and tone

### Why this style is the right choice

The logo already suggests:

- Dark green as a core brand color
- Black / charcoal for seriousness
- Gold accent for prestige
- A shield-like structure that implies protection and reliability

That means the website should not look:

- overly playful
- futuristic
- neon
- experimental
- overly decorative

Instead, it should look:

- mature
- official
- polished
- modern
- service-oriented

## 5) Color Palette

The logo suggests a strong and suitable palette already.  
The website should build from it, not fight against it.

### Primary Colors

#### Dark Green

Use as the main brand color.

- Suggested: `#0E5A43`

#### Charcoal / Near Black

Use for headers, text, and strong contrast.

- Suggested: `#1E1E1E`

#### Gold Accent

Use sparingly for premium highlights and separators.

- Suggested: `#C8A96B`

#### Soft Background

Use for main page background and section contrast.

- Suggested: `#F8F9FA`

### Supporting Colors

#### Muted Gray

- Suggested: `#6B7280`

#### Border Gray

- Suggested: `#E5E7EB`

#### Success Green

- Suggested: `#16A34A`

#### Soft Beige / Warm Sand

Useful for alternative section backgrounds.

- Suggested: `#F4EFE6`

### Color Usage Rules

- Green = brand identity, primary CTAs, icons, active states
- Charcoal = headings, strong text, footer, navigation
- Gold = thin accents only, not dominant
- White / Off-white = breathing space
- Gray = support text and subtle UI details

### Important Rule

Do not overuse gold.  
Gold should feel like an accent, not a full theme.

## 6) Typography

Typography must support authority and clarity.

### Arabic Fonts

Recommended:

- **Cairo**
- **IBM Plex Sans Arabic**
- **Alexandria**

Best choice:

- **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** for a clean modern corporate feel
- **Cairo** if you want a slightly stronger and more formal look

### English Fonts

Recommended:

- **Inter**
- **Manrope**
- **Space Grotesk** (for select bold headings only)

Best choice:

- **Inter**

### Typography Hierarchy

#### H1

Very large, confident, and short.

- 1 to 2 lines maximum
- Strong weight
- Tight line-height

#### H2

Section headlines should be clear and structured.

#### Body

Use readable sizes, generous line-height, and strong contrast.

### Recommended Scale

- H1: `clamp(2.8rem, 6vw, 5.5rem)`
- H2: `clamp(2rem, 3vw, 3rem)`
- H3: `1.25rem - 1.5rem`
- Body: `1rem - 1.125rem`
- Small text: `0.875rem`

### Typography Principles

- Arabic must remain highly legible
- Avoid extremely thin weights
- Use bold only where needed
- Maintain clean spacing between letters and lines
- Keep English subtitles subtle and secondary

## 7) Layout System

### Recommended Layout Style

Use a **clean corporate grid** with strong section separation.

### Structure Rules

- Use wide margins
- Keep the content centered
- Use clear section spacing
- Avoid clutter
- Avoid dense multi-column chaos
- Prefer 1 or 2-column structures

### Suggested Grid

- Desktop: 12-column grid
- Tablet: 8-column grid
- Mobile: single-column stack

### Layout Behavior

The landing page should move in this order:

1. Hero
2. Trust signals
3. Services
4. Why choose us
5. Countries
6. Process
7. Statistics
8. Testimonials
9. CTA
10. Footer

## 13) Motion / Animation

Animations should be subtle.

### Good Motion

- fade in
- slight upward transition
- smooth hover
- gentle section reveal
- subtle card lift

### Avoid

- excessive parallax
- flashy neon animation
- high-speed motion
- distracting transitions

### Motion Tone

The movement should feel:

- calm
- premium
- controlled
- professional

---

## 14) Spacing Rules

Spacing is a major part of the premium feel.

### Rules

- Use generous vertical spacing between sections
- Give headlines enough breathing room
- Make cards feel open, not cramped
- Keep line length readable

### Suggested Spacing

- Section padding: `96px - 140px`
- Card padding: `24px - 36px`
- Button padding: medium to large
- Gap between elements: consistent and intentional

---

## 15) Responsive Behavior

### Desktop

- wide layout
- strong hero image
- visible CTA and trust bar
- 2-column blocks where useful

### Tablet

- compress spacing carefully
- preserve hierarchy
- avoid tiny text

### Mobile

- single-column stack
- sticky CTA optional
- large buttons
- simplified hero content
- fast loading images

### Mobile Priority

On mobile, the user should immediately see:

- the company name
- the core service
- the CTA
- trust proof

---

## 16) UI Tone to Avoid

Avoid these styles:

- Brutalism
- Cyberpunk / futuristic
- Heavy glassmorphism
- Loud neon palettes
- Playful startup visuals
- Overdecorated luxury styles
- Too much shadow or blur
- Dense dashboard-like layouts

These would reduce trust and make the brand feel less official.
